# 0052: Use case: a caretaker robot for seniors at home, directed by their family

- Date: 2026-11-14
- Status: accepted
- Source: Alex, Sat afternoon (P-1)
- Agreed by: Alex

Miguel.exe is a caretaker robot in a senior's home. The senior's children use an iOS app to ask the robot to do things for their parent, for example bring their medication or fetch a glass of water. The robot still listens to the senior directly by voice and gesture (0003, 0012).

## Alternatives

From the brainstorm in `04-product.md`: fetch-and-carry helper, medication reminder and delivery, fall check-in, find-my-things, telepresence. This one combines fetch-and-carry and medication delivery, and adds the family as the people who direct it remotely.

## Reversibility

Hard after tonight: the skills, the app and the demo script are built around it. How it carries things without a gripper, how the family app reaches the robot, and the main track are still open (P-5 to P-7, T-17, P-2).
